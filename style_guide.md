# Style Guide

## Language
Natural Hinglish in Roman script.

Use Devanagari strategically when ElevenLabs pronunciation benefits from it.

Examples:
- door → डोर
- phone → फोन
- screen → स्क्रीन
- app → ऐप
- button → बटन
- notification → नोटिफिकेशन

Do not transliterate every English word.

## Voice
Cinematic storyteller.
Narration-led.
Selective acted dialogue.

## Rhythm
Short paragraphs.
Clear pauses.
Emotion tags.
SFX cues.
Strong hooks.

## Visual
Anime-style cinematic motion comic.
High consistency.
Static artwork + camera motion + compositing.
