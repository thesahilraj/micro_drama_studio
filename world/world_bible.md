# WORLD BIBLE — SETTING, ECONOMY, AND SYSTEM RULES

## 1. WORLD SETTING: MODERN SHANGHAI (MAGIC CAPITAL / 魔都)

The story takes place in a hyper-realistic, ultra-competitive modern Shanghai (Magic Capital):
- **Socio-Economic Landscape**: Extreme polarization between struggling young graduates renting shoebox apartments and the untouchable elite living in multi-hundred-million-yuan suburban compounds.
- **Atmospheric Visuals**: Towering cyberpunk glass skyscrapers along the Huangpu River (The Bund / Lujiazui), contrasted with bustling street stalls, cramped residential alleyways, and grand neoclassical gated manors.

## 2. THE SYSTEM ENGINE: TRILLION SUBSIDY (万亿补贴)

### 2.1 Operating Rules
1. **Un-installable Reality Override**: The application appears directly in the smartphone OS. It cannot be deleted, closed, or suppressed.
2. **Midnight 00:00 Refresh Cycle**: New inventory is listed every night precisely at 00:00:00 AM.
3. **Subsidy Scale**: High-tier physical assets (supercars, villas, entire residential apartment towers, commercial plazas) are subsidized by 99.999999%, pricing them at 0.1 RMB to 10 RMB.
4. **Legal Materialization & Grounded Bureaucracy**:
   - The system does not merely "spawn" items magically in mid-air.
   - It manipulates global banking ledgers, land registries, corporate ownership databases, and courier networks.
   - Official real estate deeds, keys, and contracts are delivered through legitimate channels (courier drops, physical fobs in pockets, sales office deed transfers).
5. **Irreversible Ownership**: All acquired assets possess 100% legal backing with zero tax liabilities or bureaucratic loopholes.
