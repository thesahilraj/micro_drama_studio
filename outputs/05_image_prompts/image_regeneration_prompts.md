# IMAGE REGENERATION & TROUBLESHOOTING PROMPTS

If an image fails during manual image generation (e.g. AI gives Lin Fan a suit too early, distorts the Porsche butterfly doors, or misinterprets the 120 property deeds), use these tuned corrective prompts.

---

## 1. COMMON FIX PROMPTS

### FIX-01: Lin Fan Accidental Luxury Clothing Drift
- **Issue**: Image model gives Lin Fan a tuxedo, suit, or expensive gold jewelry in Sequences 1–10.
- **Corrective Tag**: `(wearing a cheap casual worn grey zip-up hoodie over a plain white cotton t-shirt and blue jeans:1.4), (understated ordinary graduate clothing:1.3), no suit, no tie, no watch, no luxury clothes`.

---

### FIX-02: Porsche 911GT1-98 Door Geometry Failure
- **Issue**: Doors open horizontally like standard sedan doors instead of rising vertically.
- **Corrective Tag**: `(aerodynamic vertical butterfly doors lifted diagonally upward into the air:1.5), (gullwing butterfly open doors:1.4), open cockpit, carbon fiber door frame, no normal car doors`.

---

### FIX-03: 120 Deeds Clutter / Paper Drift
- **Issue**: Model renders loose paper or books instead of official red Chinese property certificates.
- **Corrective Tag**: `(stacks of hardcover deep red property ownership certificates:1.4), (embossed golden national crest and gold Chinese typography on red booklet:1.3), no scattered white papers, no blank sheets, official real estate certificates`.

---

### FIX-04: Suet Jade Color Drift
- **Issue**: Model renders green jade or cheap emerald glass instead of white suet jade with maroon veins.
- **Corrective Tag**: `(translucent pure mutton-fat white suet jade disc:1.5), (warm milky white jade stone:1.4), (subtle natural deep maroon and cinnabar veins on rim:1.3), center circular hole, no green jade, no emerald, no glass`.

---

### FIX-05: 90-Degree Bow Failure (Song Shuming / Zhou Wancai)
- **Issue**: Character only nods head or shakes hands instead of the full formal 90-degree Japanese/East Asian corporate bow.
- **Corrective Tag**: `(formal 90-degree deep bow, torso bent completely perpendicular to legs:1.5), (deep respectful corporate bow:1.4), hands at sides, head facing floor, extreme deference, no handshakes, no standing tall`.
