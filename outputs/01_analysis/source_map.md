# SOURCE MAP — EPISODE 1 (CHAPTERS 1–8)

## 1. SOURCE OVERVIEW
- **Source Arc**: Chapters 1 through 8 (System Awakening & First Asset Cascade)
- **Primary Protagonist**: Lin Fan (林凡) — 23-24 y/o broke graduate turned trillion-tier hidden powerhouse.
- **Core Engine / Concept**: "Trillion Subsidy System" (万亿补贴 / Wan Yi Bu Tie) — Daily 00:00 midnight flash-deals subsidizing 9-figure real estate, hypercars, and apartment blocks down to 0.1–10 RMB.
- **Genre**: Urban System / Face-Slap / Micro-Drama / High-Pacing Modern Shenhao (神豪).
- **Target Episode Runtime**: ~42–48 minutes (Anime Cinematic Motion Comic).
- **Linguistic Tone**: Gritty, fast-paced, high-voltage Hinglish narrative with punchy punchlines, dramatic tension, and visceral face-slap payoffs.

## 3. CORE STORY THEMES & HOOK DYNAMICS
1. **The Asymmetry of Perceived Wealth**: Lin Fan is dressed in 50-yuan casual Taobao wear while wielding billions in verified tier-1 assets.
2. **Instant Gratification & Face-Slap Cadence**: Every antagonist/doubter (Mercedes Driver -> Liao Yingying -> Landlady Zhao) confronts him with condescension, only to be struck down by indisputable institutional deference (fob activation, 90-degree bows, 120 deeds).
3. **Causality & Grounded Logic**: Assets arrive with realistic legal bureaucracy (deeds issued by sales office, physical keys popping into pockets, deeds delivered in courier boxes, registered agency agreements).
