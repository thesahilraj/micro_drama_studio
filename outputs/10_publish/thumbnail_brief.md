# THUMBNAIL CREATIVE BRIEF — YOUTUBE THUMBNAIL (16:9)

A high-CTR, viral anime thumbnail design manual for human graphic designers or AI compositing.

---

## 1. COMPOSITION & VISUAL HIERARCHY
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (`1280x720` or `1920x1080`)
- **Left Side (The Dominant Protagonist)**:
  - Lin Fan (`CHAR-001`) in his signature grey hoodie and white tee, looking directly at the viewer with an icy, calm, handsome smirk.
  - In his right hand, he holds a glowing red property deed booklet with gold Chinese lettering and a sparkling golden Porsche key fob.
- **Center / Background**:
  - The gleaming pure white Porsche 911GT1-98 with butterfly doors lifted high vertically, cyan LED lights flaring.
  - Behind the car, the majestic neoclassical columns of Dongjiao No. 1 Villa under a dramatic sunset sky.
- **Right Side (The Defeated Antagonists)**:
  - Landlady Zhao (`CHAR-006`: purple floral blouse) collapsing onto her knees in comical terror, eyes bulging, hands on head.
  - Showroom Director Liao Yingying (`CHAR-003`: crimson blazer) weeping with hands covering her face.
  - In between, two massive open cardboard courier boxes overflowing with stacks of red property deeds.

---

## 2. GRAPHIC TEXT OVERLAYS & COLOR ACCENTS
- **Main Bold Typography**:
  - Top Left: `₹ 0.1 MEIN 600 CRORE?!` (Bright Yellow font with thick black outline and red drop shadow).
  - Bottom Right: `120 FLATS KA REVEAL! 😱` (White and Neon Cyan with glowing stroke).
- **Color Palette**: High-contrast contrast between golden yellow highlights, deep royal blue shadows, vibrant red deed books, and bright emerald cash glow.
