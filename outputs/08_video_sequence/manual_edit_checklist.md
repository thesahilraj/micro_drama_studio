# MANUAL EDIT CHECKLIST — VIDEO ASSEMBLY EXECUTION GUIDE

Step-by-step checklist for the human editor during video assembly.

---

## 1. PRE-EDIT PREPARATION
- [ ] Create project file at `3840x2160` (4K) or `1920x1080` (1080p), `24.00 FPS`.
- [ ] Import 40 generated NanoBanna image assets (`IMG-001` through `IMG-040`).
- [ ] Import exported ElevenLabs voice stems (`VOX-NARR`, `VOX-001` through `VOX-012`).
- [ ] Import sourced SFX files (`SFX-001` through `SFX-025`) and BGM tracks (`BGM-01` through `BGM-12`).

---

## 2. TIMELINE ASSEMBLY STAGES
- [ ] **Stage 1 (Voice Track Alignment)**: Place Narration and Acted Dialogue onto primary audio tracks. Set dialogue baseline to `-6 dB` and narration to `-4 dB`.
- [ ] **Stage 2 (Image Rough Cut)**: Place `IMG-001` through `IMG-040` on Video Track 1 matching the sequence timeline durations.
- [ ] **Stage 3 (Motion & Camera Movement)**: Apply continuous Ken Burns pan/zooms (100% -> 112% scale) to all still images. Apply 2.5D separation to `IMG-010` (butterfly doors) and `IMG-017` (90-degree bow).
- [ ] **Stage 4 (SFX & Impact Timing)**: Align sound effects precisely to visual action points (e.g. key drop, door lift, bow slam, boxes landing, key ring drop).
- [ ] **Stage 5 (BGM Layering & Ducking)**: Lay background score tracks on Audio Track 3. Apply auto-ducking (-6 dB to -10 dB) whenever narration or acted dialogue is speaking.
- [ ] **Stage 6 (Color Grading & Overlays)**: Apply subtle film grain (2–3%), lens flares, and dust mote particles.
- [ ] **Stage 7 (Export & Final Polish)**: Export master video using H.264/H.265, High Profile, 40 Mbps bitrate, 48 kHz 24-bit stereo audio.
