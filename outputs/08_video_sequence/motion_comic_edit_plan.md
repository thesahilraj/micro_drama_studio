# MOTION COMIC EDIT PLAN — SOFTWARE-NEUTRAL ASSEMBLY SPECIFICATION

A comprehensive, software-neutral manual editing guide (compatible with Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, After Effects, Final Cut Pro) for assembling static anime artwork into a dynamic cinematic motion comic.

---

## 1. MOTION COMIC VISUAL TECHNIQUES MATRIX
1. **2.5D Parallax Layering**: Separate character foreground from background plate using AI depth map or Photoshop layer cut; apply subtle independent panning (foreground moves 1.3x faster than background).
2. **Ken Burns Dynamic Panning & Zooming**: Continuous micro-zooms (100% -> 115% scale over 8–12 seconds) to keep static images feeling organic and alive.
3. **Speed-Ramps & Impact Shakes**: Add camera shake (0.2s duration, 15px amplitude) on high-impact beats (90-degree bow, door slam, deed boxes dropping).
4. **Cinematic Light Leaks & Volumetric Particle Overlays**: Overlay subtle floating dust particles, light rays, or cyan LED flares to enhance depth.
