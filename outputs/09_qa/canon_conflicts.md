# CANON CONFLICTS — RESOLUTION LOG

This document tracks ambiguities or minor discrepancies in the source chapters and documents their canonical production resolutions.

## 1. DETECTED SOURCE DISCREPANCIES & RESOLUTIONS

### Conflict 01: Lin Fan's Name Typo in Chapter 4
- **Source Observation**: In Chapter 4 (lines 139 and 171), the text briefly refers to "Lin Mo" instead of "Lin Fan".
- **Canonical Decision**: Resolved as "Lin Fan" (`CHAR-001`). Lin Fan is the invariant protagonist name across Chapters 1–3 and 5–8.

---

### Conflict 02: Real Estate Agency Name Variation
- **Source Observation**: In Chapter 6, the agency is referred to as "Lover's Agency" (爱家 / Love Home), while in Chapter 8 (lines 142, 173), it is also mentioned as "Lianjia" (链家).
- **Canonical Decision**: Standardized as **Lover's Real Estate Agency (Love Home)** with Manager Zhou Wancai as General Manager. The dual mention reflects common real estate brokerage terminology in Chinese web novels.

---

### Conflict 03: Unit Count vs Building Layout in Junyue Huating
- **Source Observation**: Chapter 6 mentions "Building No. 1 has two units... 120 houses", while Chapter 7 (line 210) notes "Building No. 1 has four bedrooms and two living rooms".
- **Canonical Decision**: Building 1 consists of 2 stairwell/elevator units containing a total of 120 hardcover residential apartments, each apartment featuring a standard 4-bedroom layout suited for co-living/rental leasing.
