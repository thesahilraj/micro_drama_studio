# ADAPTATION NOTES — PACING, TENSION, AND DIALOGUE DYNAMICS

## 1. COMPRESSION & PACING STRATEGY

### 1.1 Prose Compression to Cinematic Pacing
- **Source Prose Redundancies**: The original novel text repeatedly re-explains basic concepts (e.g. why Shanghai prices are high, why renting is hard, why Porsche is expensive).
- **Adaptation Action**:
  - Convert repetitive internal thoughts into high-impact, rhythmic Hinglish voiceover narration.
  - Cut redundant explanations; show the financial disparity visually through clean UI overlays (Bank balance: ₹5,220 vs Villa: ₹600,000,000).
  - Accelerate travel/driving transitions using dynamic split-screen cuts, engine rev audio, and motion-blur pans.

### 1.2 Dialogue-to-Narration Ratio
- **Target Ratio**: 65% Cinematic Hinglish Narration, 35% High-Stakes Acted Dialogue.
- **Rule**: Narration establishes context, inner psychology, sensory atmosphere, and world stakes. Dialogue only triggers during emotional conflicts, reveals, commands, and face-slaps.

## 2. DRAMATIC TENSION & FACE-SLAP BEAT STRUCTURE

Every confrontation follows the **5-Stage Shenhao Impact Formula**:

```
[1. SETUP & DISDAIN] ──> Antagonist/Onlooker judges Lin Fan solely by his Taobao clothes.
[2. ESCALATION]       ──> Antagonist threatens, mocks, or attempts public humiliation.
[3. THE UNQUESTIONABLE PROOF] ──> Key fob click / 90-degree executive bow / Stacks of 120 legal deeds.
[4. THE PARALYSIS / SLAP]     ──> Instant physical and psychological collapse of the antagonist.
[5. COLD SYSTEMIC PAYOFF]     ──> Lin Fan does not engage in petty brawling; he enforces structural, legal, and financial ruin.
```

## 3. LINGUISTIC & VOICE DIRECTION (HINGLISH DIALECT)

1. **Natural Urban Hinglish**:
   - Use dynamic conversational urban Hindi + English blend.
   - Example: *"Bhai, tension mat le... gaadi bilkul theek hai, tu nikal."*
   - Avoid archaic Hindi (*kripaya*, *vyavastha*) and avoid pure robotic English.
2. **ElevenLabs TTS Pronunciation Anchors**:
   - Use strategic Devanagari only for tricky English words that TTS might mispronounce or over-anglicize:
     - `door` → `डोर`
     - `phone` → `फोन`
     - `screen` → `स्क्रीन`
     - `boss` → `बॉस`
     - `key` → `की`
3. **Pacing & Emotional Tags**:
   - Every dialogue and voiceover line in the final script will be marked with precise emotion tags (`[calm]`, `[shocked]`, `[sneering]`, `[whispering]`, `[roaring]`, `[dramatic]`, `[sarcastic]`) to ensure exact ElevenLabs voice performance.
