# Audio Bible

## Narrator
Cinematic storyteller.

## Character voices
Each recurring character gets a stable voice direction:
- age impression
- gender presentation
- energy
- pacing
- emotional range
- speaking style

## ElevenLabs
Scripts must be ready for direct voice generation.

## SFX
Only suggestions are generated automatically.
Final sourcing and Audacity placement remain manual.

## BGM
Use sparingly.
Prefer mood-driven cues over constant music.
