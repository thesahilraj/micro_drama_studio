## 2. PRONUNCIATION & PHONETIC RULES FOR ELEVENLABS

1. **Loanword Transliteration Strategy**:
   - Words requiring Devanagari guidance:
     - `door` → `डोर`
     - `phone` → `फोन`
     - `screen` → `स्क्रीन`
     - `app` → `ऐप`
     - `button` → `बटन`
     - `Porsche` → `पोर्श`
     - `key` → `की`
2. **Numbers & Currencies**:
   - Write out figures phonetically in Hinglish to ensure exact delivery:
     - `0.1 yuan` → `ek dimaag ghumane wala 10 paise ka coin` / `point one yuan`
     - `600 million` → `Chhe Sau Crore` (or `600 Million RMB`)
     - `80 million` → `Assi Crore` (or `80 Million RMB`)
     - `120 houses` → `Ek sau bees flats`
